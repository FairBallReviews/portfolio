BrandLifecycleGrowth

From Zillow knock-off to a rebrand

Homes.com, CoStar Group
Situation

The product had a muddy brand and a weak value proposition, reading to the market as a knock-off clone rather than a destination of its own.

Move

Case 03
PartnershipsMeasurementExecutive

Challenging a program with no measurable results

Hilton Worldwide
Situation

I inherited a roughly $700K per year partner channel running at negative attributable ROI, next to a legacy program I suspected was trading rate down $20 to $50 a night with nothing to show for it.

Move

I shifted spend to print, CTV, TV, radio, and digital retargeting under consistent branding, then ran a six-month cross-team value study to prove the legacy program's real effect before defending the change to leadership.

Result

ROI moved from 0.6:1 to 3.4:1 in eight months, the study showed +$14 average RevPAR and +154% YoY partnership revenue, and I wrote the CEO talking points for the Franchise Owners conference.

I like solving new and different problems, I stay for the long-term results, and I work as well with the people above me as with the people I lead.
